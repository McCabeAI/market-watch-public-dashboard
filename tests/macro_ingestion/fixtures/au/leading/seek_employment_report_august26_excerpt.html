National Insights:

  • Job ads rose for the first time in over a year, up 0.5% from July.

  • Applications per job ad continue to climb, up 0.4%, and are again at peak levels. 

State and Territory Insights:

  • All states rose m/m, led by Victoria where volumes increased 1.1%. 

  • Only Western Australia has recorded a rise in job ads y/y, up 1.8% from August 2025. 

Industry Insights:

  • A 3.6% rise in Hospitality & Tourism led overall job ad growth m/m. 

  • The Industrial and Construction sectors are experiencing the most annual growth, with Engineering, Mining, Resources & Energy and Trades & Services among the six industries to record y/y rises.

SEEK Chief Economist, Dr Blair Chapman says:

“For the first time since mid-2025, demand for workers rose modestly, with broad-based growth in Victoria the major contributor. 

“The large hiring industries of Trades & Services, Healthcare & Medical and Manufacturing, Transport & Logistics all posted solid monthly gains nationally, but it was the 3.6% jump in Hospitality & Tourism ad volumes that really moved the dial.

“As our data is seasonally adjusted, the significant rise in Hospitality & Tourism demonstrates unexpectedly high demand, over and above what we would expect at this time of year as venues look toward the warmer months. 

“Even as job ads turn around, applications per ad have not fallen. They rose again month-on-month to peak levels, highlighting the continued fierce level of competition for the jobs on offer.

“Separately, demand for AI-related skills also continues to rise, with Agentic AI and Ethics and Governance skills both more than doubling year-on-year.”

AI Insights 

The rise in demand for AI-related skills picked up pace in August, up 3.9% m/m and 66.2% y/y (though this is slower than the 88.0% y/y recorded in January). 

The majority of AI mentions are within Information & Communication Technology, where references appear in 14.1% of job ads, followed by Marketing & Communications (7.9%) and Advertising, Arts & Media (5.6%).
