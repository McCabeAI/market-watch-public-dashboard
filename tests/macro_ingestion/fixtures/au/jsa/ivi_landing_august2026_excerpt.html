What is IVI?

The Internet Vacancy Index (IVI) is a monthly count of online job advertisements compiled by Jobs and Skills Australia. Data are available by occupational groups, skill level groups, state or territory and by regional areas.

What can IVI be used for?

IVI results can provide insights into the Australian labour market by answering questions such as:

  • Is labour demand broadly increasing or decreasing?
  • Which regions of Australia have the highest demand for a particular occupation?
  • Which skill levels are in high demand by state and territories?
  • How are occupational trends changing over time?

IVI data provides a detailed and timely picture of online job advertisement activity. These can inform employment, education, and economic policy decisions. These data can help identify trends, acting as a proxy for recruitment activity, and can complement other economic indicators to assess the overall health of the labour market.

Key points

In seasonally adjusted terms, job advertisements increased by 0.3% (or 560 job advertisements) in August 2026 to stand at 212,400. Advertisement numbers continue at elevated levels, with around 25% more advertisements in the labour market presently than compared with the monthly average for 2019.

Methodology

The IVI methodology outlines how online job advertisement data are collected, processed and classified to produce reliable labour market indicators. It explains the sources of data, seasonal adjustment techniques and the regional and occupation structures used for analysis. The page also includes important guidance on interpreting IVI results.
