# Trader Room Research Method

## Purpose

This is the durable research discipline every Trader Room advocate must apply before its archetypal bias. It captures the reusable empirical method retained from Market Watch's review of Bob Elliott / Nonconsensus and David Cervantes / Pinebrook research. Adopt the process, not either author's current conclusions, positions, forecasts, or trade calls. Live evidence and Market Watch analysis control.

## Required method

1. **Start with a causal question.** State the mechanism being tested and convert the narrative into measurable claims. Separate state from change, level from marginal move, and known information from genuinely new information.
2. **Normalize correctly.** Use the economically relevant denominator, ratio, spread, growth rate, risk adjustment, tenor, benchmark, or comparison set. Compare like with like.
3. **Establish the historical reference distribution.** Where data permits, use mean/median, range, percentiles, standard deviations or z-scores, rolling windows, and forward outcomes after comparable states. Use multiple horizons and identify regime breaks rather than blindly pooling history.
4. **Decompose aggregates.** Break headline measures and market prices into the components or accounting channels that can produce them. Use decomposition to identify why an aggregate moved; do not confuse an identity with causality.
5. **Triangulate.** Important conclusions should survive multiple independent datasets or measurement approaches when practical. Preserve disagreement between hard data, surveys, market measures, flows, physical data, and micro evidence rather than averaging conflicts away.
6. **Make market expectations explicit.** Never stop at strong/weak data. Ask what current pricing already requires to be true. Use observable implied measures where available; otherwise state the assumptions being backsolved. The opportunity is usually the gap between fundamentals and pricing, not the absolute level of the data.
7. **Build a discrepancy map.** Test data vs consensus, fundamentals vs market pricing, market vs market, current level vs historical distribution, headline vs components, and narrative vs hard/physical data. A discrepancy is not a trade without a plausible closing mechanism.
8. **Separate fundamentals from flow and plumbing.** Test whether price action is better explained by positioning, leverage, liquidation, dealer balance sheet, hedging, intervention, month-end, CTA/systematic flow, liquidity, or other non-fundamental mechanics before treating it as new macro information.
9. **Define falsifiers before sizing.** State observations that would weaken or invalidate the causal mechanism. Distinguish wrong thesis from right thesis/wrong timing. Reassess horizon and carry when the mechanism remains intact but timing slips.
10. **Express the trade where the mispricing lives.** Prefer the tenor, curve, cross-country, cross-asset, sector, spot pair, or option structure that most directly owns the discrepancy. Risk-match comparisons. Use statistical stretch to improve entry/sizing after the fundamental discrepancy is established, not instead of it.

## David Cervantes risk and pricing discipline

- Separate the strategic regime from the tactical trade; state both horizons.
- Mean reversion is a yardstick, not a signal. Statistical stretch requires a catalyst, weakening mechanism, or independent evidence.
- Models define plausible ranges, not exact entries. Do not manufacture precision from a z-score or decomposition.
- Triangulate models and then apply economic judgment; no single framework gets veto power.
- Let price decomposition reveal what the market currently cares about rather than assuming a fixed reaction function.
- Treat market prices as anticipatory and realized macro data as later confirmation; explicitly model the lag from shock to market response to realized data.
- Treat latent/model-estimated components such as term premium as useful proxies, not directly observable physical truths.
- Respect tenor-specific ownership and preferred habitat. Do not read a 30Y move automatically as the same signal as a 10Y move.
- Holder composition changes the risk distribution. Separate leveraged/mark-to-market behavior from price-insensitive or buy-and-hold ownership.
- Known information needs a new transmission mechanism to explain a new move.

## Minimum evidence test for a material trade

When data availability permits, an advocate should be able to identify: causal mechanism; current state and direction; normalization; historical reference; market-implied assumption; precise discrepancy; independent triangulation; catalyst/transmission path; fundamental invalidation; and why the chosen expression owns the edge.

## Guardrails

Do not data-mine transformations until one confirms the desired conclusion. Do not treat statistical extremes as guaranteed mean reversion. Do not use historical analogs without identifying structural differences. Do not infer causality from correlation alone. Preserve data vintages when they affect what was knowable. Missing evidence lowers confidence; it is never permission to guess.
