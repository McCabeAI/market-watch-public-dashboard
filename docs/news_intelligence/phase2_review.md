# Phase 2 design review

Verdict: **REVISE**

The cap removal, receipt contract, module split, and recall classes are implementable. Two contradictions in the authoritative spec would miss a success criterion or fail a locked test if coded as written. Phase 1B’s worked examples close the scoring hole only if this spec adopts them; today it calls those notes evidence and itself authoritative.

## Blocking issues

**Filtered overlay output is not the public news list.** `overlay_accepted_research` replaces `families["news"]` (`scripts/overnight/accepted_news.py` around the `items: list(news)` assignment). `assemble_dataset` then sets `agent_research` from the raw packet supplement (`scripts/overnight/assemble.py` line 159), and `persist_accepted_public_news_from_assembly` writes `agent_research["news"]` (`accepted_news.py` lines 138–149), not `families["news"]["items"]`. `apply_overnight_news_refresh` renders `dataset["agent_research"]` only. Vietnam and India live on that packet list, not in the live candidate stream, so a suppress/hold drop that touches only the overlay’s family copy leaves them in `accepted_public_news.json` and in the HTML. Section 11’s residual-risk line treats “when the supplement is applied” as that drop; the apply path does not use the dropped list. Trader review does, because `scheduled_output` reads the overlay families, so the desk packet and the public artifact would diverge. Design change: after the overlay builds the kept news list (shallow copies, suppress and hold removed, input packet and `families` not mutated, `central_bank_research` not filtered, kept order unchanged), `assemble.py` must store that list on a copy of `agent_research["news"]` before `persist_accepted_public_news_from_assembly`. `assemble.py` is not on the off-limits list. Do not edit `apply_overnight_news_refresh.py`, `publish.py`, workflows, ACP, or `data/overnight/accepted_public_news.json`. Envelope keys stay `schema_version`, `type`, `overnight_run_id`, `as_of`, `summary`, `news`, `central_bank_research`, `source`.

**The show threshold and the closed disposition table do not score the locked fixtures.** `show` requires `transmission_score >= 10`. Points are channel weights plus a named closed-list asset plus shock. The Hormuz acquisition fixture (`tests/test_live_news_acquisition.py` “crude bounced” / Hormuz offer) contains no closed-list ticker and not the words conflict, haven, or blockade, so a literal pass is `channel_oil_energy` 5 plus a shock of 0, 2, or 3: score 5, 7, or 8. Score 5 matches none of show (10+), assess (7–9), hold (ambiguous rule), or suppress (penalty). Section 4.2 keeps only show and assess, so the diplomacy baseline (“Hormuz reopening” / “easing tensions”) at oil-only 5 is omitted and `test_hormuz_bloomberg_outranks_stale_diplomacy` fails its `assertIsNotNone(diplomacy)`. China Q3 plus crude demand is `channel_china_spillover` 5 plus shock, at most 8, so section 9’s `show` / `china_commodity_spillover` fails the same way. Phase 1B examples (c) and (d) reach 15 and 10 only by inferring assets and a second channel; this spec never adopts those assignments. Design change: make Phase 1B worked examples (a)–(e) normative point assignments. Live Hormuz refusal: `oil_energy_tot` 5, `geopolitics_haven` 4, inferred Brent+USD (`asset_named` 3), shock 3, score 15, `show`, `oil_supply_shock`; diplomacy reopening: same channels, `asset_named` 2, shock 2, score 13, `show`; tie-break +3 only on the live Bloomberg row (`recency` within 12h of merge `when`, `novelty`, Bloomberg), salience 18 vs 13. China stimulus with crude demand: `china_spillover` 5, two inferred spillover assets (+3), shock 2, score 10, `show`, `china_commodity_spillover`. Any fired channel with score below 7 is `assess`, never dropped. Examples (a) and (b) still win over tokens: Vietnam GDP is `local_non_g10_macro` suppress; the India import-bill summary stays `incidental_link_only` suppress even though it says oil, Middle East, and conflict. Missing `published_at` does not earn recency. That keeps the live Hormuz id inside `top_market_driver_candidates` when collect merges the current accepted baseline.

## Non-blocking notes

Checked and holding:

- Per-source `[:8]` and `_cap_items(16)` are actually removed (sections 1 and 4.2). `presentation_caps` stay 6 and 3. The HTML 6/3/12 slices stay in off-limits `apply_overnight_news_refresh.py`. The new bounds are a newest-60 ceiling per surface and a post-score cap of 24, not a hidden `[:8]`.
- No ACP doc, workflow, credential, paid API, article-HTML GET, or CNBC surface is required. New surfaces are one metadata URL each (three extra Bing RSS queries plus six official RSS feeds). Failures stay per-receipt.
- Receipt dict stays the seven keys. `partial` stays “one of bloomberg/reuters failed and the other ok.” Acquire keys stay `mode`, `cutoff`, `partial`, `receipts`, `candidates`. Merge keeps `items` and `research_supplement`. Bloomberg fixture path drops still yield `candidate_count` 3 and `result_count` 1 if `result_count` is the number of candidates that receipt emits. `safety_ceiling_applied` stays off the receipt.
- Import table is acyclic if it wins: `news_relevance` is stdlib only, `news_cluster` does not import `live_news`, `news_diagnostics` does not import `live_news` at import time. The sentence that parsers call `attribute_country_codes` fights that table. Stamp codes in `live_news` after `parse_surface`, or import that function inside the parse function. A module-top import from `news_sources` back into `live_news` is the cycle.
- Stage-1 credit and AI-financing clauses do not need Fed, oil, or CPI tokens. CoreWeave’s headline (CDS, debt, distress) is `show` / `ai_capex_credit` from the section 3.4 bullet (credit 5 + `credit_spreads`/`UST` + acute shock) on an injected Reuters or Bloomberg row. Do not add CNBC.
- Chicago Fed / CFNAI is explicitly show-if-score≥10 else assess, never suppress or hold, so overlay can keep `FRESH_NEWS_ITEM` then `ACTIVITY_ITEM` without a sort. “Light” channel weight does not change that.
- Diagnostics stages `discovery_failure`, `relevance_rejection`, and `presentation_suppression` are different buckets. Hold, stage-1 misses, path/window drops, and `official_title_filter` sit in `relevance_rejection`. The 24-cap and display-cap omissions sit in `presentation_suppression`.
- Accepted envelope keys are unchanged. Central-bank rows are excluded from the filter. Shallow-copy-then-attach does not mutate inputs if the copy is made first. “Preserve dict identity” must not be read as writing `transmission` onto the packet dict.
- Scope stays a scorer plus a fixed URL list. No model, embedding, or paywalled body. FT/WSJ, Bloomberg markets firehose, RBNZ HTML, and unfiltered SEC 8-K stay deferred.

Clarifications that are not new features, once the two blocking edits land:

- Isolate every new surface fetch. A test fetcher that raises on non-Bing/non-Reuters URLs must become `failed` for that receipt only and must not change `partial` or drop the Bloomberg Hormuz candidate. Offline returns before any fetch.
- Keep `_verification_from_edges`, Bloomberg display-edge preference, `_same_event`, and the string `distribution_weight` on candidates. `market_salience.distribution` stays `high` or `standard`.
- Rows cut by the newest-60 ceiling are not yet scored, so they are not `presentation_suppression`. Record them as a ceiling flag with a stage of their own or under `discovery_failure`, and do not pretend the shadow report sees HTML `[:6]`/`[:3]`/`[:12]` drops.
- BoJ/BoE keep-token `statement` will pass non-policy titles. Prefer the monetary-policy phrases already listed; do not treat every BoJ statistics title as a keeper.
- Bare sole `gdp` at stage 1 drops a wire headline before a reason code exists. Overlay and the Vietnam/India tests must call stage 2 on the full row anyway. Do not widen stage 1 to every `gdp` hit; the Phase 1C Anthropic “US GDP” line is not a Phase 2 anchor, and opening that token brings Vietnam back through discovery.
- Scores under 7 with a real channel need the assess rule in the blocking edit. Do not invent a fifth disposition.
