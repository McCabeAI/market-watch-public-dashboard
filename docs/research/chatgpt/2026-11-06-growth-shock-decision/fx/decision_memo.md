## Preferred action, falsifier, limitations

Preferred action: no trade in AUDJPY and no trade in AUDNZD. `prefer_neither`.

The AUDJPY no-trade is falsified if a later ECB reference trades through the 2026-10-15 low 109.17499075101739 because AUDUSD fell, with USDJPY unchanged or lower, while WTI and Brent are not higher.
