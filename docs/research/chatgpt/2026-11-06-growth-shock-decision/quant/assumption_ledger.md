# Assumption ledger (quant only)

## Units

- **Yield histories in the source map** use **basis points (bp)** in `series[].unit == "bp"`; e.g. 446.0 bp reads as **4.46%**. Daily changes there are already in bp (difference of consecutive levels).
- **CORRA and SOFR implied rates** in `tradable_curves` are **percent**; **price = 100 − implied_rate**. CRAH27 and SR3H7 checks are recomputed in `results.json` → `identity_checks`.
- **Implied-rate moves** for forward PnL: \((\text{level} - \text{reference}) \times 100\) → **bp**.
- **CORRA book strip (parallel average)**: `dv01_usd_per_bp = 10_000 × (notional_usd / 1e8)` (**book_identity_parallel_average**; not the single quarterly CRA contract factor).
- **SR3 (single quarterly contract)**: `dv01_usd_per_bp = 25 × (notional_usd / 1e6)` (used only for SR3 rows).
- **Scale note (informational)**: 10_000 per bp per USD 100m vs 2_500 per bp per USD 100m is a **4×** ratio on notionals; identities are not “corrected” across products.

## Sign convention

- **Positive `pnl_usd`** means the priced expression (long receive, long EURUSD, etc.) **makes money** at the hypothetical level; **losses are negative**.
- **`loss_usd_positive`** = `max(0, -pnl)`.
- **Directional PnL never nets financing.** Financing is a separate **positive cost** in USD.

## Horizons (do not mix)

- **Financing**: **70 calendar days**, ACT/360, rate from `funding_context_sofr` (reconciled to 3.94% on 2026-11-04 in `identity_checks`).
- **Vol scaling** in yardsticks: **√h** with **h = 40 or 60 trading days** (not 70 calendar days).

## What was not netted

- Directional scenario sums **exclude** accrued seat funding history and **exclude** netting financing into rate or FX marks.
- **EUR carry sensitivity** is reported separately; it is **not an FX forward**, **not** a spot forecast, and **not** added to spot-directional P&amp;L.

## Preferred set (from judgment `preferred == true`)

- `corra_cut_50m` — proposed USD 50,000,000 CORRA receive (parallel-average strip identity).
- `eur_hold_5m` — proposed USD 5,000,000 EURUSD long.
- `sofr_h7_flat` — flat / zero notional SR3 expression.

Inputs and full outputs: `inputs.json`, `results.json` (generated by `calc.py`).
